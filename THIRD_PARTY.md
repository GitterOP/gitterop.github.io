# Template attribution

This site is a deliberately small adaptation of [Academic Pages](https://github.com/academicpages/academicpages.github.io), based on revision `8c57e1ae5da379512eb11165257fba02664b8531` (2026-11-12).

The default layout, shared masthead structure, navigation conventions, and base-path include derive from that template. The page layouts and CSS have been tailored to this personal site. Academic Pages derives from Michael Rose's Minimal Mistakes theme. Its MIT license is retained in [LICENSE](./LICENSE).

Research figures are extracted from the corresponding articles, identified and linked in their captions. Article PDFs and CV retain their own authorship and applicable terms; the template license does not relicense these documents.
